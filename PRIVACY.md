# NexyBlock Privacy Policy

NexyBlock does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

- **No network access of its own.** The extension makes no requests to any server. Its content security policy blocks outgoing connections (`connect-src 'none'`).
- **No telemetry or analytics.** Nothing about your browsing, YouTube activity or settings is sent anywhere.
- **Settings stay local.** Your on/off choices are saved in Firefox's extension storage (`storage.local`) on your device.
- **Optional statistics and diagnostics are off by default.** If you turn them on, they are only counters (for example "requests blocked"), kept in memory for the current session. They contain no URLs, video IDs or page content, are never saved to disk or uploaded, and are cleared when you turn them off or restart the extension.
- **Page access is used only for filtering.** NexyBlock reads YouTube player responses only to remove ad fields before the page receives them. Response contents are not kept after processing.

Questions: open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
